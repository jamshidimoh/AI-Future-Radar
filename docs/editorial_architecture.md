# AI Future Radar — Four-Lane Editorial Architecture

The radar is organized as four editorial lanes. Lanes are editorial responsibilities, not merely topic tags.

## Lane 1 — AI Core
Question: What materially changed in AI itself?

Focus: models, capabilities, reasoning, multimodality, AI coding, memory/context, training/inference advances, and consequential AI product/research developments.

Selection: normal editorial ranking, quality floor, mission coverage and diversity contracts.

## Lane 2 — Technical Trend
Question: Where are AI architecture, infrastructure and engineering patterns moving?

Focus: Agent Harness, Agent Framework, MCP, Context Engineering, Tool Calling, Computer Use, agentic architecture, inference serving, KV Cache, speculative decoding, MoE, quantization, model compression, multimodal/transformer architecture, AI infrastructure, accelerators, RAG, knowledge graphs, evaluation, observability, gateways, protocols, runtimes, SDK/API architecture.

Selection: independent technical score and eligibility. The Normal News score floor is not applied. Maximum one publication per period. It does not consume Normal or Mind/Science/Future capacity.

## Lane 3 — Mind / Science / Future
Question: What do AI developments mean for science, mind, cognition and the future?

Focus: consciousness, cognition, neuroscience, philosophy of mind/science/technology, BCI, biological intelligence, AI+biology/genetics, AI+quantum/physics, robotics, scientific discovery and future-oriented technology analysis.

Selection: independent relevance/value logic and protected diversity. Generic science or philosophy without a meaningful AI/future connection is excluded.

## Lane 4 — Voices / Perspectives
Question: What are credible, influential people in these fields saying about the direction and implications of these changes?

Focus: substantive interviews, talks, lectures, podcasts, debates, expert views, researcher/founder perspectives and high-value quotations. A person is not enough: the content must contain a substantive, mission-relevant idea or interpretation.

Selection: independent Voices score based on substantive voice signal, person relevance, source authority, analytical value and recency. Excludes Reddit/community/aggregator/arXiv paths. The lane must not inherit the Normal News score floor.

## Cross-lane rules

1. Each lane has its own eligibility gate, score, ranking window, quota and publication marker.
2. Lane selection happens before shared final delivery gates.
3. A story should have one primary editorial lane for publication; cross-lane overlap is resolved by lane priority and publication identity.
4. Shared contracts remain global: deduplication, source safety, language QA, Telegram delivery, state persistence and final publication integrity.
5. Education remains a separate scheduled content contract and is not one of the four editorial lanes.
6. Tier-0 critical/priority protections remain orthogonal to the four editorial lanes.
7. Normal News must not absorb or suppress a valid Technical Trend, Mind/Science/Future or Voices signal merely because its Normal score is below the Normal floor.
8. Diversity is enforced within and across lanes so the radar does not become four copies of the same story.

## Editorial objective

The four lanes together answer four complementary questions:

**AI Core — What changed?**
**Technical Trend — How is the technology evolving underneath?**
**Mind / Science / Future — What does it mean for science, mind and the future?**
**Voices / Perspectives — How are credible experts interpreting the direction?**

The objective is signal detection and editorial understanding, not maximum news volume.
