# Protected Mind / Ideas / Voices Lane

The production portfolio is adaptive, with an overall operational safety ceiling of eight news items per run. The protected Mind/Ideas/Voices lane is a candidate role within the shared portfolio, not an additive publication quota. A qualifying item competes on evidence, score, freshness, duplication and mission diversity; it does not bypass quality gates or create extra capacity.

The lane is intended for substantive, evidence-grounded material involving influential technology and AI leaders and CEOs, scientists, theorists, public intellectuals, sociologists, and scientifically grounded futurists; interviews, podcasts, talks, lectures, firesides, conversations, and discussions with such people; and AI-relevant work on consciousness, philosophy of mind, philosophy of science, philosophy of technology, cognitive science, neuroscience and the brain, genetics/genomics/synthetic biology, foresight/futures research, society and technology, and related interdisciplinary domains.

The lane participates in the unified final portfolio rather than taking a separate set of slots. All candidates remain subject to source authority, evidence, freshness, semantic deduplication, Persian-language quality, grounding, Telegram safety and the publication contract. Domain and lane roles help measure diversity and relevance; they do not bypass the shared adaptive score policy or eight-item safety ceiling.

Reddit, community, and aggregator sources remain excluded. Generic mentions of a person are not sufficient unless the item carries a configured registry/person signal or a qualifying specialist theme.
