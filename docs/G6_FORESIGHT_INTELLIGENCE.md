# G6 — Foresight Intelligence

Status: IMPLEMENTED — VALIDATION PENDING

G6 synthesizes persisted trend, temporal, convergence, and evidence signals into auditable drivers and comparative scenarios.

## Model

A driver combines momentum, persistence, convergence strength, supporting evidence, contradiction ratio, and uncertainty. Scenario records retain the contributing driver identities and explicitly label their probability value as a comparative proxy rather than a calibrated probability.

The scenario set includes multiple structurally different futures: acceleration, continuity, fragmentation, disruption, and stall. The configured subset is selected deterministically from evidence-conditioned scores.

## Safety of interpretation

Contradictory evidence increases uncertainty rather than being discarded. The system does not claim that a deterministic score constitutes a forecast probability.

## Integration policy

G6 is disabled by default and publication-decoupled. It does not modify editorial ranking, eligibility, quotas, Telegram delivery, or current production behavior.

## Non-goals

G6 does not perform autonomous external-world prediction, replace expert judgment, or activate production decisions. G7 introduces measurement/shadow mode; G8 is the first controlled integration gate.
