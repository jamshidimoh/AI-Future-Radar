# Education stream restart and source policy (2026-11-03)

## Restart

The Telegram Education sequence has been reset to lesson 1 at the user's request. The previous completion list is intentionally cleared; no lesson is considered completed in the new cycle until Telegram confirms a message ID and the completion state is committed. The configured curriculum remains in its existing order: foundation, curriculum modules, emerging terminology, then expansion lessons.

## Source policy

- Prefer primary standards, official technical documentation, major university research centers, peer-reviewed research, and authoritative public-sector or research reports.
- Prefer independently verified 2026 publication/update evidence when source authority and topic relevance are comparable.
- Keep 2025+ verified sources eligible. Do not force a 2026 citation for stable foundational definitions when a canonical source is more appropriate; add 2026 evidence for current state or implications where relevant.
- A year declared in YAML is not proof of a 2026 update. Only detected publication/update metadata or narrowly reviewed exact-URL overrides count as dated-current evidence.
- Require at least two reachable, current sources from independent organizations. A Tier 1 source is preferred; an unverified or unreachable URL never satisfies the requirement.
